# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Directors and lab managers of independent clinical laboratories in Mexico, Colombia, and broader LATAM. 1–10 person operations. Currently managing orders on paper or Excel. Not tech-savvy — they bought a lab to run a health business, not to manage software. Decision-makers who feel the pain daily.

## Product Purpose

Lab2Next replaces paper-based clinical lab workflows: order intake, patient registration, QR portals, WhatsApp result delivery, referral tracking, multi-branch dashboards. Positioned against big-lab chains that hoard technology — Lab2Next gives independent labs the same capability on day one, no IT department needed.

## Brand Personality

Confiable, cercano, moderno. Professional without being clinical. Warm without being unprofessional. The brand should feel like a trusted local partner who also happens to know what they're doing technically.

## Anti-references

- Epic / Meditech / SAP Health — cold, institutional, corporate-medical
- Generic WordPress/Wix templates — cheap, disposable feel
- Overdesigned fintech dark-mode SaaS — too alien for the target audience
- Cluttered Latin American government health portals

## Design Principles

1. **Show the lab what tomorrow looks like** — concrete demonstrations of the product beat abstract promises. UI mockups, real numbers, specific features over generic claims.
2. **Respect earned not assumed** — the audience is skeptical of tech promises. Every claim needs a proof point nearby.
3. **Warmth at scale** — the copy and design should feel like a conversation with someone who's been in a lab, not a pitch deck.
4. **Clarity over cleverness** — Spanish-speaking busy operators. No jargon, no metaphors that don't land in LATAM context.
5. **Motion serves story** — animations (scroll-driven roadmap, how-it-works steps) exist to illustrate workflow, not to decorate.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. prefers-reduced-motion respected (roadmap already handles this). Spanish as primary language throughout. No English-only UI.
